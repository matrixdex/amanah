Shipping & Returns

UK delivery for a flat £10 per order.

Last updated: November 2026

Where we deliver

We currently deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom only. You enter your delivery address at checkout.

Delivery charge

Delivery is a flat £10 per order, whatever you order. The charge is shown in your cart and at checkout before you pay.

When your order ships

Orders ship within two working days. Delivery times after dispatch depend on the courier and your location. If your order has not arrived when you expect it, email us at info@amanahealth.uk and we will look into it.

Returns

If you change your mind, you can return unopened products in their original condition within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price.

  • Email us at info@amanahealth.uk with your name and the items you would like to return, and we will tell you where to send them.
  • Return postage is paid by you, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.
  • We refund you to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the return.

Opened products

Our products are food and wellness products. For health protection and hygiene reasons, we cannot accept the return of products that have been opened or whose seal has been broken, unless they are faulty.

Damaged, faulty or wrong items

If something arrives damaged or faulty, or is not what you ordered, email us at info@amanahealth.uk as soon as possible, ideally with a photo. We will send a replacement or a full refund, including the delivery charge.

This policy does not affect your statutory rights under UK consumer law, including the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
