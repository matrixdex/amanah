Who we are

Amanah products are crafted with the best natural ingredients to support gut health, immunity, and longevity. Each product formula is rooted in naturopathy to provide health benefits. Amanah products are manufactured locally in the United Kingdom with potent natural ingredients.

Our name

Amanah is an Arabic word for trust: something placed in your care that you look after honestly. It is the standard we hold ourselves to, in what goes into our products and in how we treat the people who buy them.

Our promise

  • Every ingredient listed. The full ingredients are on every pack and on every product page.
  • Made in the United Kingdom.
  • Against animal testing.
  • Nothing you don't need. Clear labels, simple formulas and honest descriptions.
