FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you deliver?

Currently, our delivery is limited to the UK. Delivery is a flat £10 per order. Orders ship within 3 working days.

When will my order be sent?

Orders ship within 3 working days. Delivery times after that depend on the courier and your location. See Shipping & Returns.

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. For health and hygiene reasons we cannot accept opened products unless they are faulty. See Shipping & Returns.

How do I pay?

You pay securely through Stripe, our payment provider, using a card or one of the other payment methods shown at checkout. Stripe collects and processes your payment details. Amanah Health Products never sees or stores your card details.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. The payment form is provided by Stripe and your card details go directly to Stripe over an encrypted connection. Stripe is certified to PCI DSS Level 1, the highest level of certification for card payments.

Where are your products made?

Our products are made in the United Kingdom by Amanah Health Products Limited.

Where can I find the ingredients?

The full ingredients are listed on every pack and on each product page. Overnight Breakfast Muesli contains gluten and nuts. Always check the ingredients if you have an allergy or sensitivity.

Can I use your products if I am pregnant or taking medication?

Please speak to a healthcare professional before use if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, or managing a medical condition. See our Disclaimer.

How do I contact you?

Email us at info@amanahealth.uk. Our postal address is on our contact page.
