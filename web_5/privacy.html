Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: we collect your name, email and delivery address so we can send your order. Your payment details are collected by Stripe, our payment provider, not by Amanah Health Products. We do not sell your information or use advertising trackers.

Who we are

This website and shop are operated by Amanah Health Products Limited ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in the United Kingdom. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You can contact us at info@amanahealth.uk or by post at Amanah Health Products Limited, P.O. Box No. 79414, London N12 2GU, United Kingdom.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your delivery address
  • your billing country and postcode, where the checkout asks for them
  • the products you ordered, the amounts paid and the date of your order

We also collect what you choose to send us when you email or write to us, and your email address if you subscribe to our updates.

Payment details are collected by Stripe

Payments are handled by Stripe, our payment provider. Your card number and other payment details are entered into a secure form provided by Stripe and go directly to Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your payment details; Amanah Health Products Limited does not see, receive or store them. We only receive confirmation that a payment was made, with the amount and limited details such as the type of card. Stripe also uses information about your device and payment to prevent fraud. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your order, including sending you messages about it. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns, refunds and complaints. This is necessary for our contract with you, or in our legitimate interests in looking after our customers.
  • To keep financial records that the law requires, for example for tax. This is necessary to meet our legal obligations.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our shop secure. This is in our legitimate interests and yours.
  • To send you occasional updates, only if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with companies that help us run the shop, and only as much as they need:

  • Stripe, to take payments and prevent fraud.
  • Our courier and shipping software provider, to print labels and deliver your order. They receive your name, delivery address and, where needed for delivery updates, your email address.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website.
  • Our email provider, to send and receive emails.
  • Our accountants and professional advisers, and authorities such as HM Revenue & Customs, where the law requires it.

Cookies and similar technologies

  • Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage so that it is still there when you move between pages. It stays on your device and is not sent to us until you check out.
  • Stripe sets cookies when the checkout loads, to keep payments secure and prevent fraud.
  • Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Our hosting provider processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics that track you across other websites. Because we only use storage that is strictly necessary for the shop to work, we do not show a cookie banner.

International transfers

Some of our providers, such as Stripe, Cloudflare and Google, may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: six years after the end of the financial year of the order, as UK tax law requires.
  • Emails and correspondence: as long as needed to deal with your query, and no longer than two years afterwards.
  • Your email address for updates: until you unsubscribe.

Keeping it safe

Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to your information is limited to the people and providers who need it to fulfil your order.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • ask us to delete your information, where we do not need to keep it by law
  • object to or restrict how we use your information
  • ask for your information in a portable format
  • withdraw consent to updates at any time

To use any of these rights, email info@amanahealth.uk. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Our shop is for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy: info@amanahealth.uk.
